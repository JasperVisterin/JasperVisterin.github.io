In this article I explain the need for understandable data visualization together with t-SNE. The article elaborates on the t-SNE framework for visualizing complicated high-dimensional datasets and an example of how a telecom company can use data science techniques to optimize its services. It illustrates how crucial data science is in our modern information-driven society.

Data visualization is the key to uncovering the fingerprint of data.
