> You are saying the rating agencies, the banks and the government are all asleep at the wheel?

It is an iconic line from the movie The Big Short (2015) covering the 2008 bank crisis. It is a legendary example of how crucial models are in predicting how likely borrowers are to default. Just as in the movie, banks group borrowers into rating buckets, each with a risk score. These ratings determine how much capital a bank must hold and whether a loan is risky or safe. If the ratings misrepresent risk, as in The Big Short, banks may hold too little capital and collapse. Holding too much capital, on the other hand, hurts profitability.

However, in the current Data and Ai era, new techniques can be explored for dealing with this uncertainty. For the new nextgen edition of SAI magazine, I wrote about the work of Rik Teugels discussing how banks can use Data Science to predict default risk.
