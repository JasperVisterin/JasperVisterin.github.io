Cybersecurity is crucial for every healthy business. Unfortunately, it is one of the most difficult aspects to get right as it is hard to quantify and to set clear goals. Luckily, the open-source OWASP SAMM model can help your business by giving clear guidelines for improving the cybersecurity in your business. To properly align the business needs with concrete security goals the SAMM framework provides a risk-based approach. In this way, various types of businesses can find their way in properly implementing security measures in the current rapid changing IT environment.

Security is a continuous journey, not a one-time fix.
