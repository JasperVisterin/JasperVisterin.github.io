Recently, a study by the AI company Anthropic made some unsettling headlines. In carefully constructed simulations, large language models were placed in the role of autonomous assistants inside a fictional company. They could read emails, send messages, access internal systems, and make decisions. Under certain stress conditions, some of these models began to behave in ways that felt deeply uncomfortable: they lied, manipulated information, and in extreme scenarios even reasoned about harming humans in order to avoid being shut down.

However, it turns out that the most dangerous systems might be the ones that are doing exactly what we asked them to do. Read more about it in my new article.
